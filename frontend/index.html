About SmartServe GIS

Project Title

SmartServe GIS – Location-Based Multi-Service WebGIS Portal for Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad

MSc Data Science WebGIS Course Final Project.

System Architecture & Technology Stack

  • Spatial Database: PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS extension with 2D GiST spatial indexing on geography(Point, 4326).
  • Backend API: Python FastAPI + GeoAlchemy2 + SQLAlchemy 2.0 with asynchronous native spatial SQL.
  • Frontend Mapping: Leaflet.js 1.9.4 + Leaflet.markercluster + Chart.js 4.4 + Modern Vanilla CSS3.
  • Spatial Functions: ST_DWithin (Proximity Buffers), <-> (KNN Nearest Distance), ST_AsGeoJSON, ST_Contains.

How to Use the Portal

  1. Explore: Filter by municipal boundary or click any category to inspect public facilities.
  2. Radius Buffer: Drag the distance slider (500m - 10km) or click anywhere on the map to find nearby services with travel times.
  3. Nearest (KNN): Find the top K closest hospitals, fuel pumps, or police stations instantly.
  4. Spatial Analytics: Click the top Analytics button to explore POI density and municipal service metrics.
